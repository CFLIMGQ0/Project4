Examination-level multimodal multi-label classification, which jointly analyzes an ordered image sequence and a paired report to recognize coexisting abnormalities, is commonly used for clinical examinations such as gastrointestinal endoscopy. However, existing methods typically use fixed, uniformly spaced positional encodings for sampled images during sequence modeling and apply global fusion to image and report features, producing a shared representation. These design choices may misrepresent image acquisition relationships and obscure label-specific evidence, weakening image-context modeling and multimodal evidence integration and reducing the reliability of coexisting abnormality recognition.

\cntranslation{%
检查级多模态多标签分类通过联合分析有序图像序列和配对报告来识别共存异常，常用于胃肠镜检查等临床检查场景。然而，现有方法通常在序列建模阶段为采样图像使用固定且等间隔的位置编码，并对图像和报告特征进行全局融合，形成共享表征。这些设计可能无法准确表示图像采集关系，并掩盖标签级证据，从而削弱图像上下文建模和多模态证据整合能力，降低共存异常识别的可靠性。
}

Unlike video or volumetric imaging data with explicit temporal or spatial coordinates, endoscopic snapshots are selectively saved during examinations, leaving acquisition order as the only available positional reference. Positional modeling for such discrete sequences remains underexplored. Existing methods follow two routes. Index-driven methods transform supplied indices, as in ComRoPE~\cite{yu2025comrope} and VideoRoPE~\cite{wei2025videorope}. Content-adaptive methods introduce input-dependent positional or attention mechanisms based on image content, as in PaTH~\cite{yang2025path} and DAPE V2~\cite{zheng2025dapev2}. However, sampled-index distances may misrepresent examination relationships: neighboring images can receive far-apart positions, whereas distant images can receive overly close positions, as illustrated in Fig.~\ref{fig:position_mismatch}. Index-driven methods still encode these relationships through index distances. Content-adaptive methods add visual cues but may retain distorted index spacing as their reference, changing how positional information is used rather than correcting the reference itself. Consequently, the original spacing mismatch may persist in subsequent modeling. The key challenge is to reconstruct positional references from visual changes while preserving acquisition order.

\cntranslation{%
不同于具有明确时间或空间坐标的视频和三维影像数据，内镜图像是在检查过程中由医生选择性保存的，因此采集顺序成为唯一可用的位置参照。针对这类离散检查序列的位置建模研究仍然不足。现有方法主要沿两条路线展开。索引驱动方法对给定位置索引进行变换，代表方法包括 ComRoPE~\cite{yu2025comrope} 和 VideoRoPE~\cite{wei2025videorope}。内容自适应方法则根据图像内容引入与输入相关的位置或注意力机制，代表方法包括 PaTH~\cite{yang2025path} 和 DAPE V2~\cite{zheng2025dapev2}。然而，采样索引之间的距离可能无法准确表示检查序列中的关系：实际相邻的图像可能被赋予相距很远的位置，而实际相距较远的图像却可能被赋予过近的位置，如图~\ref{fig:position_mismatch}~所示。索引驱动方法仍然通过索引距离编码这些关系。内容自适应方法虽然引入了视觉线索，但可能仍以存在偏差的索引间距为参照，改变位置信息的使用方式，却没有纠正位置参照本身。因此，原有的间距错配仍可能在后续建模中保留。关键问题是在保持采集顺序的同时，根据视觉变化重建位置参照。
}

Beyond sequence modeling, related studies address examination-level multimodal multi-label recognition through cross-modal interaction and label-aware representation learning. SAIF~\cite{zhang2025saif} and MMTF~\cite{zhong2026mmtf} enhance image--text interaction, whereas MambaML~\cite{zhu2025mambaml} and Wang et al.~\cite{wang2025granularity} learn label-specific visual or semantic representations. However, these approaches generally do not jointly organize examination-specific visual and textual evidence around each label. Because a single report is paired with the entire examination, different labels may depend on different images and report passages and may exhibit structured co-occurrence relationships; global fusion can mix their evidence without explicitly modeling these correspondences or interactions. Label-aware visual or semantic representations alone may not identify relevant passages in an examination-specific report, and the importance of such passages can vary across labels and examinations. These limitations motivate label-centric multimodal fusion with explicit coexistence reasoning.

\cntranslation{%
除了序列建模，相关研究主要通过跨模态交互和标签感知表征学习处理检查级多模态多标签识别。SAIF 和 MMTF 增强图文交互，MambaML 和 Wang 等人则学习标签特异性的视觉或语义表征。然而，这些方法通常没有围绕每个标签联合组织当前检查中的视觉与文本证据。由于一份报告与整个检查配对，不同标签可能依赖不同图像和报告片段，同时还可能存在结构化的共存关系；全局融合却可能混合这些标签的证据，无法显式建模这些证据对应关系或标签交互。仅有标签感知的视觉或语义表征，也未必能定位当前检查报告中的相关片段，而这些片段的重要性还会随标签和检查样本而变化。这些局限促使我们构建带有显式共存推理的标签中心多模态融合方法。
}

 To this end, we develop Acquisition-aware Label-centric Multimodal Multiple Instance Learning (ALM-MIL), an examination-level multimodal multi-label framework built upon Acquisition-aware Contextual Position Encoding (ACPE) and Label-Centric Coexistence Fusion (LCCF). ACPE anchors sampled images to their original acquisition coordinates and constructs content-adaptive contextual positions according to visual transitions, while preserving the monotonic structure of the acquisition sequence. LCCF further organizes multimodal evidence around individual labels: it first aggregates label-specific visual evidence, models higher-order interactions among coexisting labels through hypergraph reasoning, retrieves label-relevant textual evidence from the paired report, and finally adaptively controls its contribution through label-wise gated fusion. Together, ACPE and LCCF enable the model to better represent acquisition relationships among multiple images while aligning examination-level image–report information with label-level prediction. Extensive experiments across private clinical cohorts and public datasets demonstrate the effectiveness and generalizability of ALM-MIL.

\cntranslation{%
为此，我们构建了采集感知的标签中心多模态多实例学习框架（Acquisition-aware Label-centric Multimodal Multiple Instance Learning，ALM-MIL），用于检查级多模态多标签分类，其核心由采集感知上下文位置编码（Acquisition-aware Contextual Position Encoding，ACPE）和标签中心共存融合（Label-Centric Coexistence Fusion，LCCF）组成。ACPE 以采样图像的原始采集坐标为锚点，并根据图像之间的视觉变化构建内容自适应的上下文位置，同时保持采集序列的单调结构。LCCF 则进一步围绕各个标签组织多模态证据：它首先聚合标签特异性的视觉证据，通过超图推理建模共存标签之间的高阶关系，随后从配对报告中检索与各标签相关的文本证据，并通过标签级门控融合自适应控制其贡献。ACPE 与 LCCF 的结合，使模型既能够更准确地表达多张图像之间的采集关系，又能够将检查级图文信息与标签级预测进行对应。我们在多个私有临床队列和公开数据集上的大量实验验证了 ALM-MIL 的有效性与泛化能力。
}

Our main contributions are summarized as follows:

本文的主要贡献总结如下：

\begin{itemize}
    \item We identify and investigate the mismatch between sampling-index distances and actual spatial relationships in discrete examination images. This perspective shifts attention from improving the encoding of supplied indices to examining the validity of the positional reference itself.

    \cntranslation{%
    我们指出并研究离散检查图像中，采样索引距离与实际空间关系之间的错配问题。 这一视角将关注点从改进给定索引的编码方式，转向审视位置参照本身的合理性。
    }

    \item We propose ACPE, which learns content-adaptive intervals from visual transitions while preserving acquisition order and sequence span.

    \cntranslation{%
    我们提出 ACPE，根据视觉变化学习内容自适应的图像间距，同时保持采集顺序和整体序列跨度。
    }

    \item We develop LCCF to organize image and report evidence per label, model label coexistence with hypergraph reasoning, and adaptively fuse label-specific text.

    \cntranslation{%
    我们提出 LCCF，围绕各个标签组织图像与报告证据，通过超图推理建模标签共存关系，并自适应融合标签相关文本。
    }

    \item We evaluate ALM-MIL on clinical and public datasets, including classification, missing-image robustness, and component ablations.

    \cntranslation{%
    我们在临床数据集和公开数据集上评估 ALM-MIL，考察其分类性能、图像缺失鲁棒性和各模块的作用。
    }

\end{itemize}
